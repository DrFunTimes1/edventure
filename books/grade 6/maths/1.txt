PATTERNS IN MATHEMATICS (SUMMARY)

WHAT IS MATHEMATICS
Math studies patterns and why they exist. Patterns appear in nature, daily life, space, games, and technology. It is used in science, engineering, economy, and real life.

NUMBER PATTERNS
All 1s: 1,1,1…
Counting: 1,2,3…
Odd: 1,3,5… Even: 2,4,6…
Triangular: 1,3,6,10… (built by adding 1+2+3+…)
Squares: 1,4,9,16… (n×n)
Cubes: 1,8,27… (n×n×n)
Virahanka: 1,2,3,5,8… (sum of last two)
Powers: 2^n, 3^n sequences.

DOT VISUALS (meaning only)
Square dots: arranged in equal rows and columns forming a perfect square grid (n×n dots).
Triangle dots: arranged in rows increasing by 1 each step (1,2,3,4…) forming a triangular stack.

Same number can form different shapes (e.g., 36 works as square and triangle).

RELATIONS
Odd sums → perfect squares: 1, 1+3, 1+3+5… = n²
Counting sum → triangular numbers.
Powers of 2 partial sums: 1, 3, 7, 15… (+1 gives 2,4,8,16…)

SHAPES
Polygons: sides 3,4,5,6…
Complete graphs: K2, K3, K4…
Stacked squares/triangles grow layer by layer.
Koch snowflake: 3,12,48,192… (×4 each step)

SUMMARY
Math studies patterns in numbers and shapes and connects them using rules and visual structure.
